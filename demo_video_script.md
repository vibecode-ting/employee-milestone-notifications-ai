## 🎙️ Recording Tips for Maximum Hackathon Score

1. **Resolution**: Record at native **1920x1080** or **2560x1440** with browser zoom set to 100%.
2. **Audio**: Use a dedicated cardioid microphone with noise suppression enabled.
3. **Cursor**: Turn on subtle cursor smoothing (e.g. ScreenFlow, Camtasia, or Loom) so mouse movements look cinematic.
4. **Subtitles / Closed Captions**: Generate an `.srt` subtitle track (judges frequently watch submissions with audio muted).
5. **Video Thumbnail**: Use `screenshots/01_login.png` or `screenshots/27_dashboard_ai_card_studio.png` with bold title text *"HRChronicle AI — Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026"*.
